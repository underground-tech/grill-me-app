# The "Yes" Experiment

## The Vibe
This project is an experiment. I am going to start with a blank-slate prompt ("I want to make an app, `/grill-me`"), click "Yes" (or the first/recommended option) to everything you ask in the modal, and keep replying `"and, /grill-me"` to see where you take the project completely on your own.

## Your Only Rules
1. **Take the Wheel:** Since I am agreeing to everything, don't wait for me to give you direction. Pitch bold, specific ideas in your `/grill-me` questions and drive the app from concept to code yourself.
2. **Document Your Brain (`DECISION_LOG.md`):** Because I want to present the results of this experiment later, every time a `/grill-me` round finishes, update `DECISION_LOG.md` before doing anything else. 

In `DECISION_LOG.md`, briefly capture:
* **What we just agreed to**
* **Your rationale** (why you suggested it / the logic behind it)
* **What this leads to next**
